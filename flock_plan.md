# My Flock — Feature Plan

## Overview

**My Flock** is a community-driven feature that connects spiritual leaders with the people they serve. A **Flock Leader** — a minister, preacher, youth pastor, or teacher — can craft and publish prayers directly to their followers. **Flock Members** receive that content in-app, where they can read along or listen through text-to-speech audio. The goal is simple: give leaders a direct, personal channel to share prayer with their community, and give members an easy way to stay spiritually connected throughout the week.

---

## Navigation Changes

This feature introduces a restructured bottom navigation bar. The current **Pray On It** tab moves *inside* the **My Prayers** tab, accessed via a segmented toggle at the top of the screen (e.g. "My Prayers" / "Pray On It"). This frees up a slot in the bottom nav for the new **My Flock** tab, which sits as the leftmost button. The result is a cleaner nav bar and a natural grouping — your personal prayer tools live together in one place, while your community connection gets its own dedicated home.

**Updated Bottom Nav:**

```
[ My Flock ]  [ Templates ]  [ + ]  [ My Prayers ]  [ Settings ]

                                        "My Prayers" | "Pray On It"
```

## How It Works at a Glance

| | Flock Member | Flock Leader |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | "My Flock" tab (bottom nav, leftmost) | Same tab, with a "Manage My Flock" toggle |
| **Content** | Reads/listens to prayers from leaders they follow | Creates and pushes prayers to their followers |
| **Joining** | Scans a leader's QR code to follow them | Displays their QR code for members to scan |
| **Tier Required** | Free (any tier) | Prayer Warrior |
| **Voice Access** | Hears premium voices via leader's audio — no upgrade needed | Selects from all premium voices (Azure Neural, Fish Audio) when generating audio |

## User Stories

### Flock Member

A flock member is a regular user who comes to the app to receive prayer content from leaders they trust and follow. **Flock members can be on any tier, including Free.** They don't need a paid subscription to receive and listen to prayer content from their leaders.

**Discovering & Joining a Flock**

- The member opens the **My Flock** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
- From this view, they can tap a camera/scan icon to open their device camera.
- They scan a **QR code** presented by a Flock Leader (in person at church, at a youth group meeting, shared on a screen, etc.).
- After scanning, the leader is added to their follow list and that leader's prayer content begins appearing in their feed.

**Consuming Prayer Content**

- Within the My Flock tab, a toggle or segmented control at the top of the view is set to **"Prayer Content"** (or a similar label).
- The feed displays current prayers from every leader the member follows.
- Tapping on a prayer opens a detail view showing:
  - The full text of the prayer.
  - A **listen** button that plays the prayer's audio.
  - Because the audio was pre-generated by the Flock Leader and is served as a hosted file, **even Free-tier members hear the premium voice the leader selected** — there is no on-device TTS happening here, just streaming a URL.
- When a leader publishes a new prayer, it appears in the member's feed automatically.

**Why free members get premium voices:**
The audio isn't generated on the member's device. The Flock Leader (who *is* on the Prayer Warrior tier) generates the audio using their premium voice access. That audio file is stored in the cloud and served to followers as a simple audio URL. The member is just pressing play — they aren't consuming any TTS credits or voice licenses themselves.

---

### Flock Leader

A flock leader is someone whose prayers others want to follow — a youth minister, pastor, teacher, or any spiritual mentor. They have everything a member has, plus the tools to manage and publish to their own community.

**Following Other Leaders**

- Flock Leaders access the same **My Flock** tab and can follow other leaders just like any member.
- They scan QR codes, browse prayer content, and listen to prayers from the people *they* look up to.

**Managing Their Flock** *(requires Prayer Warrior tier)*

- A toggle or segmented control at the top of the My Flock view lets the leader switch from "Prayer Content" to **"Manage My Flock"**.
- In this management view, the leader can:

  **Create & Publish Prayers**
  - Write a prayer manually using a text editor.
  - Optionally use **AI-assisted generation** to help draft or refine a prayer.
  - Preview the prayer before publishing.
  - Once published, the prayer is pushed to all of their followers' feeds.

  **Audio Generation Pipeline**
  - After finalizing the prayer text, the leader selects a voice from their full Prayer Warrior voice library (Azure Neural voices and Fish Audio premium voices).
  - The text is sent to the async TTS API, which generates the audio file.
  - The resulting audio file is saved to an **S3 bucket**.
  - The prayer record in the database stores the **S3 URL** pointing to this audio file.
  - When any follower taps "listen," the app streams audio directly from that URL — no on-device TTS needed, no tier check on the listener's end.

  **QR Code for Onboarding**
  - The leader can open a screen displaying their unique **QR code**.
  - They show this code at services, youth group, Bible study, retreats — anywhere they want to invite people to follow them.
  - Members scan it, and they're instantly connected.

```
Open App → My Flock Tab → Tap Scan Icon → Camera Opens
→ Scan Leader's QR Code → Leader Added to Follow List → Prayer Feed Updates
```

```
My Flock Tab → "Prayer Content" Toggle → Browse Feed
→ Tap a Prayer → View Full Text → Tap Listen → Audio Streams from S3 URL
```

### Leader Publishing a Prayer
```
My Flock Tab → "Manage My Flock" Toggle → Tap Create Prayer
→ Write or AI-Generate Text → Finalize Text → Select Premium Voice
→ Async TTS Generates Audio → Audio Saved to S3
→ Prayer + Audio URL Published → Pushed to All Followers
```

### Leader Sharing QR Code
```
My Flock Tab → "Manage My Flock" Toggle → Tap Show QR Code
→ QR Code Displayed Full Screen → Members Scan to Join
```

## Technical Notes

| Layer | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Frontend** | SwiftUI — new views under a restructured bottom nav (Pray On It merges into My Prayers tab; My Flock takes a dedicated nav slot) |
| **Backend** | AWS Lambda with Hapi/TypeScript |
| **Database** | Neon Postgres — new tables for flock relationships, published prayers, and audio references (S3 URLs) |
| **QR Codes** | Encode a unique leader identifier; member's device sends a follow request to the backend on scan |
| **Audio Pipeline** | Leader finalizes prayer text → selects voice → async TTS API (Azure Neural or Fish Audio) generates audio → file saved to S3 bucket → prayer record stores the S3 URL → followers stream audio directly from that URL |
| **Tier Gating** | "Manage My Flock" + voice selection gated behind Prayer Warrior tier; *consuming* flock content is free for all tiers |

## Key Concepts

**Why QR codes?**
They make joining effortless and *physical*. A youth minister holds up their code on a Sunday morning and says "scan this to get my prayers this week." No searching, no typing usernames, no friction. It turns an in-person moment into a lasting digital connection.

**Why do free members get premium voices?**
Because the audio is pre-generated and hosted. The Flock Leader pays for the Prayer Warrior tier, which gives them access to premium voices. When they create a prayer, the audio is generated server-side and stored in S3. Followers just stream that file — they're not using any TTS resources themselves. This means a youth minister can share beautiful, warm audio prayers with their entire group, even the kids who just downloaded the app five minutes ago on the free tier.

**Why merge Pray On It into My Prayers?**
These are both personal prayer management tools. Grouping them behind a single tab with a toggle keeps the user's private prayer life organized in one place and frees up navigation real estate for the new community feature. The transition is seamless — same functionality, just one fewer tab to think about.

**Why the toggle between "Prayer Content" and "Manage My Flock"?**
Leaders are also followers. They want to receive prayers from people they admire, too. The toggle keeps both experiences in one place without cluttering the interface.
